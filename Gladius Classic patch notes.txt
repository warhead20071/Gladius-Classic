v0.4.0 5/2/2026
* General Changes:
   * Made the game playable
   * Had to remove sprites due to file path issue that happens on some computers
   * Settings can now be saved to local storage
   * You now gain gold at the end of battles instead of from killing enemies. That was changed sometime after v0.1.1, but the patch notes are somewhere in the void
   * You can no longer get a tier 2 upgrade shop as your first shop in a chapter
   * Various Necromancer ai changes
   * Gladius now has a plume on his helmet
* Balance changes:
   * Gold earned per battle buffed: 25 + (stage# * 5) → 25 + (stage# * 7)
   * Enemies per wave changed: 8 → 4 + (2 * chapter)
   * Common upgrades price buffed: 25 gold → 20 gold
   * Upgrade shop refresh price buffed: 25 gold → 20 gold
   * Player exp requirement per level scaling buffed: 1.5x → 1.25x
   * Various event chance changes:
      * Battles: 35% → 40%
      * Upgrade shops: 15% → 27.5%
      * Weapon shops: 15% → no change
      * Wizard shops: 17.5% → 15%
      * Mysterious artifact: 2.5% → no change
   * Spell tomes base cost buffed: 150 gold → 100 gold
   * Spell tomes cost per level buffed: 100 + (50 * level) → 50 + (50 * level)
   * Weapons base cost buffed: 150 gold → 100 gold
   * Weapons cost per level buffed: 150 * level → 50 + (50 * level)
   * Player exp gained from killing fodder enemies buffed: 10 → 15
   * Player exp gained from killing elite enemies buffed: 25 → 40
   * Enemy level up after boss fight buffed: 3 → 5
   * Lightning bolt spell damage nerfed: 300% → 250%
   * Ball lightning spell zap hit rate nerfed: every frame (WTF) → 0.1 sec (10 frames)
   * Ball lightning ball damage nerfed: 1000% → 750%
   * Daggers attack cooldown nerfed: 0.3 sec → 0.75 sec
   * Chain lightning radius buffed: 60 → 90
   * Ball lightning projectile speed buffed: 7 → 2 (it’s a buff trust)
   * Necromancer undead summon time nerfed: 0.5 sec → 1 sec
   * Necromancer reanimation stagger counter (normal form) nerfed: 20 → 10 (mysterious artifact form unchanged)
   * Necromancer dark magic blast attack cooldown buffed: 2 sec → 1.5 sec
   * Necromancer Hp nerfed: 1000 + (450 * level) → 750 + (250 * level)
   * Necromancer reanimating attack summons changed: 1 lych (2 w/ mysterious artifact form) → 5 wisps (10 w/ mysterious artifact form) + 6 undead
   * Lych reanimation summons changed: 5 skeletons → 3 skeletons + 1 wisp
   * Skeleton move speed nerfed: 1 → 0.75
   * Player base hp regen nerfed: 5 hp/s → 3 hp/s
* Bug Fixes:
   * Fixed an issue where the player attack would not properly point at the mouse cursor on different screen sizes
   * Fixed an issue where the director would spawn more enemies than intended
   * Fixed an issue where attempting to rebind a control to spacebar would instantly click the rebind button again. Pressing spacebar now puts control rebinding on a 0.25 second cooldown
   * Fixed an issue where the shop options position would be offset if you paused and unpaused while in a shop
   * Fixed an issue where wizard shops were more common than upgrade shops. It was a mistake, there’s no way i did that on purpose
   * Fixed an issue where the Ogre would break his legs if he jumped
   * Fixed an issue where ball lightning zap would apply total damage twice
   * Fixed an issue where the weapon shop owner pulls a nintendo on you and doesn’t let you own the weapons you buy
